One-Pager 2: CNS Rosettes, Ependymomas, Embryonal Tumors & Meningioma Grading

Surgical Path (50%) + IHC/Molecular (10%) + Day 1 Spot & Day 2 SOE

1. Master Differential of the 5 CNS Rosettes & Pseudorosettes:

  • Homer Wright Rosettes (Neuroblastic/Fibrillary Core): Halo of tumor nuclei surrounding a central tangle of fibrillary neuropil processes (no lumen, no vessel). Seen in Medulloblastoma, CNS Neuroblastoma, and Pineoblastoma.
  • Flexner-Wintersteiner Rosettes (True Lumen — Retinoblastic): Ring of cuboidal cells with an apical limiting membrane surrounding a true central empty/hyaluronidase-resistant lumen. Seen in Retinoblastoma and Pineoblastoma.
  • True Ependymal Rosettes: Tumor cells arranged around a true central lumen with apical cilia/blepharoplasts. Pathognomonic for Ependymoma.
  • Perivascular Pseudorosettes: Radial arrangement of tumor cell processes tapering toward a central blood vessel creating a perivascular anuclear fibrillary zone. Classic for Ependymoma (also seen in Medulloblastoma, Central Neurocytoma, Pilomyxoid Astrocytoma, Angiocentric Glioma).
  • Pineocytomatous Rosettes: Large, irregular, confluent anuclear fibrillary meshworks (synaptophysin+) surrounded by uniform small cells in Pineocytoma.

2. Ependymal Tumors (WHO CNS5 Anatomic-Molecular Classification): Ependymomas are characteristically GFAP+ (perivascular processes), S100+, EMA paranuclear dot-like/ring-like (+), and OLIG2 (-). WHO CNS5 stratifies them into: (a) Supratentorial Ependymoma, ZFTA fusion-positive (L1CAM IHC+) vs. YAP1 fusion-positive (better prognosis, young children); (b) Posterior Fossa Group A (PFA) (infants/young children, lateral location, poor prognosis, loss of nuclear H3K27me3 driven by EZHIP overexpression) vs. Posterior Fossa Group B (PFB) (older children/adults, retained H3K27me3, favorable prognosis); (c) Spinal Ependymoma (NF2-associated vs. aggressive MYCN-amplified); (d) Myxopapillary Ependymoma (filum terminale/conus medullaris, papillary cores with alcian-blue+ mucin, upgraded from Grade 1 to CNS WHO Grade 2 in WHO CNS5 due to recurrence risk!).

Medulloblastoma, WNT-activated (Gr 4) Classic morphology; dorsal brainstem / 4th ventricle Nuclear β-catenin (+), YAP1 (+), GAB1 (-), Filamin-A (+) CTNNB1 mutation (or APC germline in Turcot) + Monosomy 6. Best prognosis (>90% 5-yr survival).
Medulloblastoma, SHH-activated (Gr 4) Desmoplastic/nodular (reticulin-free pale islands) or MBEN; cerebellar hemispheres GAB1 (+), YAP1 (+), Cytoplasmic β-catenin, p53 (stratifies TP53-wt vs. mut!) PTCH1, SMO, SUFU (Gorlin syndrome). SHH TP53-mutant has dismal prognosis (often Li-Fraumeni); TP53-wt has good/intermediate prognosis.
Medulloblastoma, Non-WNT/Non-SHH (Groups 3 & 4) Classic or Large cell/anaplastic; midline vermis Cytoplasmic β-catenin, GAB1 (-), YAP1 (-) Group 3: MYC amplification, isochromosome 17q (i17q) — worst prognosis. Group 4: i17q, KDM6A — intermediate prognosis.
AT/RT (Atypical Teratoid/Rhabdoid Tumor, Gr 4) Rhabdoid cells (eccentric nuclei, macronucleoli, pink hyaline inclusions) + embryonal areas Loss of nuclear INI1 (SMARCB1) [95%] or BRG1 (SMARCA4) [5%]; EMA+, SMA+ Biallelic inactivation of SMARCB1 (22q11.2) or SMARCA4 (19p13.2). Infants <3 yrs; rule out rhabdoid tumor predisposition syndrome.
