1. Molecular & Cytogenetic Assay Comparison (SCFHS Curriculum Molecular Table):

Assay / Platform Analytic Sensitivity (LoD) Min. Tumor Cellularity Primary Anatomic Pathology Applications & Pitfalls
Sanger Sequencing ~15–20% VAF (variant allele frequency) ≥30–40% neoplastic cells (requires H&E macrodissection) Gold standard for known single-gene point mutations/small indels; false negatives occur in paucicellular or mucinous tumors (<30% tumor cells).
Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) ~2–5% VAF (standard FFPE); <0.5% with UMI ctDNA ≥10–20% neoplastic cells Multigene panels, TMB, MSI, and RNA-seq fusion detection. Blocked by strong acid decalcification (HCl/nitric acid degrades nucleic acids — always use EDTA decalcification!).
Allele-Specific PCR / qPCR / ddPCR 0.01% – 1% VAF (ultra-sensitive) ≥1–5% neoplastic cells Hotspot detection in low-cellularity cytology/cell blocks or liquid biopsy (e.g., EGFR T790M, BRAF V600E, KIT D816V, MYD88 L265P).
FISH (Break-Apart vs. Dual-Fusion) Single-cell resolution on FFPE interphase nuclei ~50–100 intact non-overlapping tumor nuclei • Break-Apart Probe: Flanks a promiscuous gene (EWSR1, SS18, MYC, ALK, ROS1); normal = fused yellow; rearranged = split 1R1G1F signal.
• Dual-Fusion Probe: Targets two partner genes (BCR::ABL1, IGH::MYC); rearrangement = 2 yellow fusion signals.
STR Identity / Chimerism Testing Amplifies polymorphic short tandem repeats Separate DNA extraction from patient & floater Resolves suspected tissue floaters/cross-contamination in the histology lab; distinguishes Complete Mole (diandric paternal-only STRs, p57-) from Partial Mole (triploid diandric-monogynic, p57+).

2. Skeletal Muscle Biopsy Triage, Enzyme Histochemistry & Inflammatory Myopathies:

  • Fresh Specimen Triage (SOE Trap): Muscle biopsy must be sent fresh/unfixed on slightly damp saline gauze (never submerged in saline or formalin!) and snap-frozen in isopentane (2-methylbutane) cooled in liquid nitrogen (-160°C) to prevent ice-crystal artifact and preserve enzyme activity.
  • Core Histochemical Stains: (1) ATPase pH 9.4: Type 1 (slow oxidative) fibers = pale; Type 2 (fast glycolytic) = dark. (2) ATPase pH 4.3 / 4.6: Reverses staining (Type 1 = dark; Type 2 = pale). Neurogenic denervation/reinnervation causes angular esterase+ atrophic fibers, target fibers (on NADH-TR), and fiber-type grouping (loss of normal checkerboard). (3) Modified Gomori Trichrome: Highlights ragged red fibers (mitochondrial myopathies), nemaline rods, and rimmed vacuoles. (4) Combined COX / SDH: Mitochondrial defects show COX-negative, SDH-positive ("ragged blue") fibers.
Inflammatory Myopathy Key Microscopic & Topographic Hallmarks IHC / Immune Mechanism & EM Findings
Dermatomyositis Perifascicular atrophy (atrophy along periphery of fascicles), perivascular & perimysial inflammation Humoral/complement microangiopathy: CD4+ T-cells, B-cells, plasmacytoid DCs, & capillary C5b-9 (MAC) deposition; EM shows endothelial tubuloreticular inclusions.
Polymyositis / Immune Necrotizing Endomysial inflammation invading non-necrotic muscle fibers (no perifascicular atrophy) Cell-mediated cytotoxicity: CD8+ cytotoxic T-cells surrounding & invading diffuse MHC Class I-upregulated myofibers.
Inclusion Body Myositis (IBM) Adults >50y (quadriceps/finger flexors); endomysial CD8+ inflammation + basophilic rimmed vacuoles + ragged red/COX-negative fibers Steroid-refractory; vacuoles contain p62+, TDP-43+, Congo-red+ aggregates; EM shows 15–18 nm tubulofilamentous inclusions.
