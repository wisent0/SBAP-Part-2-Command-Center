One-Pager 1: WHO CNS 5th Ed. Adult-Type & Pediatric Diffuse Gliomas

Surgical Path (50%) + Molecular/IHC (10%) + Day 2 SOE

1. Etiology, Molecular Pathogenesis & Epidemiology: Adult-type diffuse gliomas are stratified by somatic point mutations in IDH1 (codon 132; >90% p.R132H) or IDH2 (codon 172; p.R172K), which convert α-ketoglutarate into the oncometabolite D-2-hydroxyglutarate (2-HG) and induce G-CIMP hypermethylation. Astrocytoma, IDH-mutant (CNS WHO Grades 2–4) peaks at 30–45 years in the frontal lobes and acquires TP53 missense mutations and ATRX loss-of-function mutations (activating alternative lengthening of telomeres). Oligodendroglioma, IDH-mutant and 1p/19q-codeleted (CNS WHO Grades 2–3) peaks at 35–50 years in the frontal cortex/white matter and is defined by unbalanced whole-arm translocation t(1;19)(q10;p10) plus TERT promoter, CIC, and FUBP1 mutations. Glioblastoma, IDH-wildtype (CNS WHO Grade 4) peaks at 55–75 years and arises de novo via canonical TERT promoter mutation, combined whole-chromosome +7/-10, or EGFR amplification. Pediatric-type high-grade diffuse gliomas are driven by histone H3 mutations: Diffuse Midline Glioma, H3 K27-altered (WHO Grade 4) in the pons/thalamus/spinal cord, and Diffuse Hemispheric Glioma, H3 G34-mutant (WHO Grade 4) in adolescent cerebral hemispheres (characteristically OLIG2-negative!).

2. Gross & Microscopic Hallmarks + Differential Diagnosis (+ / -):

  • Astrocytoma, IDH-mutant vs. Reactive Gliosis: Diffuse astrocytoma is an ill-defined mass blurring the gray-white junction with irregular, hyperchromatic, elongated ("naked") nuclei, nuclear crowding, perineuronal satellitosis, and fibrillary/gemistocytic cytoplasm (IDH1 R132H+, ATRX nuclear loss, strong p53 >10–50%). Reactive gliosis shows evenly spaced ("non-overlapping") astrocytes with symmetric stellate processes on GFAP, CD68+ macrophages, low Ki-67 (<1–2%), IDH1 R132H (-), retained ATRX, and wild-type p53.
  • Oligodendroglioma vs. Clear/Round Cell Mimics (Clear Cell Ependymoma, Central Neurocytoma, DNET): Oligodendroglioma shows monomorphic round nuclei, perinuclear halos ("fried-egg" artifact on permanent FFPE sections), branching "chicken-wire" capillaries, and microcalcifications (OLIG2+, IDH1 R132H+, ATRX retained, 1p/19q codeleted). FROZEN SECTION TRAP: Perinuclear halos are a formalin-fixation artifact and are ABSENT on frozen sections! Always use intraoperative crush smears. Clear cell ependymoma is OLIG2 (-), EMA paranuclear dot (+), IDH1 (-); Central neurocytoma is intraventricular, Synaptophysin (+), NeuN (+), OLIG2 (-), IDH1 (-); DNET is intracortical multinodular with "floating neurons" in mucin pools, FGFR1-altered, IDH1 (-).
  • Circumscribed Astrocytic Gliomas: Pilocytic Astrocytoma (WHO Gr 1; KIAA1549::BRAF fusion) shows a biphasic compact piloid (Rosenthal fibers) and loose microcystic (eosinophilic granular bodies [EGBs]) pattern; glomeruloid vessels and infarct-like necrosis do NOT upgrade it to GBM. Pleomorphic Xanthoastrocytoma (PXA, Gr 2–3; BRAF V600E + CDKN2A/B loss) is superficial/meningocerebral with pleomorphic/lipidized cells, EGBs, reticulin network, and CD34+.

1. SOE Data-Gathering:
Ask for exact anatomic site (hemispheric vs. midline thalamus/pons/cord vs. intraventricular), MRI T1 post-contrast ring-enhancement (to detect undersampling of high-grade glioma), and prior low-grade glioma or radiation history.
2. SOE Reasoning (IHC/Mol):
Order GFAP, OLIG2, IDH1 R132H, ATRX, p53, Ki-67. If IDH1+ & ATRX lost -> Astrocytoma (test CDKN2A/B for Gr 3 vs. 4). If IDH1+ & ATRX retained -> 1p/19q FISH for Oligo. If IDH-wt -> test TERTp, EGFR amp, +7/-10 + MGMT methylation.
3. SOE Decision-Making (FS):
Perform crush smear + frozen section. Intraoperatively rule out Primary CNS DLBCL (CD20+), abscess/toxoplasma, or acute demyelination where aggressive surgical resection is contraindicated!
4. SOE Professional & QC:
Verify positive internal endothelial/neuronal nuclear controls before calling ATRX or H3K27me3 loss. Issue a WHO CNS5 layered integrated report for the neuro-oncology tumor board.
