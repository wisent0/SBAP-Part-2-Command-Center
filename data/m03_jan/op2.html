One-Pager 2: Aggressive B-Cell, Hodgkin, Mature T/NK-Cell Lymphomas & SOE Lymph Node Script

Surgical Path / Heme (50%) + IHC/Flow/FISH (10%) + Day 1 Spot & Day 2 SOE

1. Aggressive B-Cell Lymphomas — DLBCL (Hans Algorithm), Double-Hit & Burkitt Lymphoma (WHO 5th Ed.):

  • Diffuse Large B-Cell Lymphoma, NOS (DLBCL): Sheets of large lymphoid cells (nuclei ≥2x normal lymphocyte or ≥1x macrophage nucleus) with centroblastic, immunoblastic, or anaplastic morphology. Hans Cell-of-Origin IHC Algorithm (30% cutoff for CD10, BCL6, MUM1): (1) CD10 (+) = GCB; (2) CD10 (-), BCL6 (-) = Non-GCB (ABC); (3) CD10 (-), BCL6 (+), MUM1 (-) = GCB; (4) CD10 (-), BCL6 (+), MUM1 (+) = Non-GCB (ABC) (worse prognosis; driven by MYD88/CD79B mutations).
  • Double-Expressor vs. Double-Hit (DLBCL/HGBL-MYC/BCL2): Double-Expressor is an IHC phenotype (MYC ≥40% + BCL2 ≥50%). Double-Hit is a genetic diagnosis requiring FISH break-apart probes for MYC (8q24) and BCL2 (18q21) (Note: in WHO 5th Ed., MYC + BCL6 rearrangements without BCL2 are excluded from the MYC/BCL2 entity!).
  • Burkitt Lymphoma (BL): Endemic (jaw/orbit, ~100% EBV+), Sporadic (ileocecal, 20–30% EBV+), or Immunodeficiency-associated. Monotonous medium-sized cells with round nuclei, multiple paracentral nucleoli, basophilic cytoplasm with lipid vacuoles on Wright-Giemsa touch prep, squared-off cell borders, and benign tingible-body macrophages creating a "starry-sky" pattern. IHC & Genetics: CD20 (+), CD10 (+), BCL6 (+), BCL2 (NEGATIVE!), TdT (-), Ki-67 ~100%; t(8;14) IGH::MYC (80%), t(2;8), or t(8;22) (lacks BCL2/BCL6 translocations!).

1. SOE Data-Gathering:
Request age, B-symptoms (fever, night sweats, >10% weight loss), localized vs. generalized lymphadenopathy, hepatosplenomegaly, CBC/smear, LDH, SPEP, and viral/immune status (EBV, HIV, HTLV-1, transplant, methotrexate).
2. SOE Reasoning (IHC/Flow/FISH):
Assess nodal pattern (follicular, paracortical, sinusoidal, diffuse) and cell size. Correlate Tier 1/2 IHC with Flow Cytometry (kappa/lambda restriction, T-antigen loss) and FISH (MYC, BCL2, BCL6, CCND1, ALK, DUSP22).
3. SOE Decision-Making (Fresh Triage):
On fresh excisional lymph node biopsy: make Wright-Giemsa touch imprints, place a portion in RPMI for Flow Cytometry & Karyotyping, snap-freeze a piece, and slice thinly (2–3 mm) into 10% NBF to prevent fixation delay and crush artifact.
4. SOE Professional & QC:
Check for prior Rituximab (anti-CD20) therapy before calling a B-cell lymphoma CD20-negative (confirm B-lineage with PAX5, CD79a, CD19) and issue an integrated WHO 5th Ed. report for the lymphoma board.
