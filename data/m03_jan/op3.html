One-Pager 3: Melanocytic Neoplasms, Cutaneous Melanoma Staging/SLN & Adnexal/Neuroendocrine Skin Tumors

Surgical Path / Dermpath (50%) + IHC/Molecular (10%) + Day 1 Spot & Day 2 SOE

1. Cutaneous Melanoma Pathogenesis, Subtypes & CAP/AJCC 8th Edition Staging Protocol: Melanoma pathogenesis is stratified by cumulative solar damage (CSD): (a) Low-CSD / Superficial Spreading Melanoma (intermittently sun-exposed skin; BRAF V600E [50%] or NRAS [20%], CDKN2A loss, TERT promoter mutations); (b) High-CSD / Lentigo Maligna Melanoma & Desmoplastic Melanoma (chronically sun-damaged head/neck of elderly with severe solar elastosis; NF1, NRAS, KIT, high TMB); (c) Acral Melanoma & Mucosal Melanoma (palms/soles/subungual; UV-independent; KIT [15–20%], CCND1/CDK4 amplifications); (d) Uveal & Blue Nevus-Like Melanoma (GNAQ or GNA11 hotspot mutations; BAP1 nuclear loss = high risk of liver metastasis). Mandatory CAP Synoptic Elements: (1) Breslow Thickness: Measured with an ocular micrometer at right angles from the top of the epidermal granular layer (or ulcer base) to the deepest invasive melanoma cell to the nearest 0.1 mm. (2) Ulceration: Full-thickness loss of intact epidermis overlying invasive tumor WITH host reactive changes (fibrin + neutrophils) and adjacent epidermal hyperplasia. (3) AJCC 8th Ed. pT Cutoffs: pT1 ≤1.0 mm (pT1a <0.8 without ulceration; pT1b 0.8–1.0 or <0.8 with ulceration); pT2 >1.0–2.0 mm; pT3 >2.0–4.0 mm; pT4 >4.0 mm. (4) Microsatellitosis: Microscopic discontinuous nest of tumor cells separated from the main tumor by normal dermis/subcutis = automatically stages as pN1c / Stage III.

2. High-Yield Melanocytic, Basaloid, Adnexal & Neuroendocrine Skin Differential Table:

Differential Pair / Entity Key Architectural & Cytologic Clues Positive IHC / Molecular (+) Negative IHC (-) & Diagnostic Traps
Spitz Nevus vs. Melanoma Spitz Nevus: Young patients, small (<6 mm), sharp lateral circumscription, symmetric epidermal hyperplasia, vertical 'raining-down' nests with clefts, dull-pink basement-membrane Kamino bodies (PAS+, Collagen IV+), maturation with depth. S100 (+), SOX10 (+), Melan-A (+), p16 (Retained diffuse +), HMB45 (stratified — top only, negative at base!); HRAS gain or kinase fusions (ALK, ROS1, NTRK1/3, RET, BRAF). PRAME (Negative <75%), Ki-67 low (<2–5% & superficial). Melanoma: Asymmetric, pagetoid scatter to granular layer, sheet-like dermal nodules, deep mitoses, PRAME diffuse nuclear + (>75%!), p16 complete loss.
SLN Benign Capsular Nevus Rest vs. Metastatic Melanoma Capsular Nevus: Bland monomorphic nevus cells strictly confined to the fibrous lymph node capsule or trabeculae (NEVER in subcapsular sinus or lymphoid parenchyma!). Metastasis: Large pleomorphic cells in the subcapsular sinus and/or lymphoid parenchyma. Both are S100 (+), SOX10 (+), Melan-A (+)!
• Capsular Nevus: p16 (+).
• Metastasis: HMB45 (+), PRAME (+), Ki-67 high (>10%).		 Capsular Nevus is HMB45 (-), PRAME (-), and Ki-67 (<1%)! Never overcall a capsular nevus rest on Melan-A/SOX10 alone—always verify location and stain with HMB45 & PRAME!
Desmoplastic Melanoma vs. Scar / Spindle SCC / MPNST Elderly head/neck; deceptively hypocellular spindle cells in collagenous stroma (mimics dermal scar!), overlying lentigo maligna in 50–70%, deep lymphoid aggregates, and prominent neurotropism. S100 (diffuse strong +), SOX10 (diffuse strong +), p75NGFR (+), WT1 (+); NF1 mutations. HMB45 (-), Melan-A (-), MITF (-), PRAME (negative/variable)! Trap: Ordering only Melan-A or HMB45 will completely miss Desmoplastic Melanoma—always order S100 and SOX10!
Basal Cell Carcinoma (BCC) vs. Trichoepithelioma BCC: Basaloid lobules with peripheral palisading, mucinous stromal retraction clefts, apoptotic bodies. Trichoepithelioma: Horn cysts, fibrotic stroma with papillary mesenchymal bodies, no retraction clefts. • BCC: BerEP4 (diffuse +), BCL2 (diffuse +), CD10 (stromal -).
• Trichoepithelioma: BerEP4 (+), BCL2 (outer basal layer only), CD10 (peritumoral stroma +), CK20 (+ Merkel cells!).		 • BCC: EMA (-), CK20 (-).
• Squamous Cell Carcinoma: EMA (+), p40 (+), BerEP4 (-). (Driven by PTCH1/SMO mutations; Gorlin syndrome).
Merkel Cell Carcinoma vs. SCLC Elderly sun-exposed head/neck; dermal/subcutaneous sheets & trabeculae of uniform round blue cells with finely stippled 'salt-and-pepper' chromatin, nuclear molding, mitoses/apoptoses. CK20 (paranuclear dot-like + in >90%!), Neurofilament (+), Synaptophysin (+), Chromogranin (+), INSM1 (+), SATB2 (+), MCPyV (CM2B4 + in 80%). TTF1 (-), CK7 (-), CD45 (-), SOX10 (-), p40 (-). (20% are MCPyV-negative, UV-induced with high TMB and TP53/RB1 mutations).
