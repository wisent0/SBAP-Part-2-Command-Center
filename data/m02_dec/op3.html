One-Pager 3: Bone & Cartilage Tumors + Maxillofacial Fibro-Osseous Lesions

Surgical Path (50%) + Day 1 Spot & Day 2 SOE

1. Mandatory Radiologic-Pathologic Topographic Rules (SOE Domain 1): Never sign out a bone biopsy without reviewing the plain radiographs and MRI: (a) Epiphyseal / Apophyseal (3 Classics): Chondroblastoma (skeletally immature <25y, open physis), Giant Cell Tumor of Bone (skeletally mature 20–45y, closed physis extending to subchondral bone), and Clear Cell Chondrosarcoma (adults 25–50y, femoral/humeral head). (b) Metaphyseal: Conventional Osteosarcoma, Enchondroma/Chondrosarcoma, Osteochondroma, Non-Ossifying Fibroma (eccentric cortical), Simple/Aneurysmal Bone Cyst. (c) Diaphyseal: Ewing Sarcoma (permeative 'onion-skin' periosteal reaction), Osteoid Osteoma (radiolucent nidus <2 cm with dense reactive cortical sclerosis), Fibrous Dysplasia ('ground-glass' matrix + 'shepherd's crook' deformity), and Adamantinoma / Osteofibrous Dysplasia (anterior tibial cortex).

Entity Age, Site & Radiology Microscopic Hallmarks & Traps IHC & Molecular Signature
Giant Cell Tumor of Bone (GCTB) 20–45y (closed physis); Epiphysis of knee/radius; eccentric lytic 'soap-bubble' without sclerotic rim Neoplastic mononuclear ovoid stromal cells uniformly mixed with huge (>50 nuclei) osteoclast-like giant cells. Denosumab Trap: Depletes giant cells & forms woven bone mimicking osteosarcoma! H3.3 G34W (nuclear + in mononuclear stromal cells!). H3F3A G34W mutation (>90%). Check serum Ca/PTH to exclude Brown tumor (G34W-)!
Chondroblastoma 10–25y (open physis); Epiphysis of femur/tibia/humerus; lytic lesion with thin sclerotic rim Polyhedral chondroblasts with longitudinal nuclear grooves ('coffee-bean'), pink chondroid matrix, giant cells, and pericellular 'chicken-wire' calcification H3.3 K36M (diffuse nuclear +!), DOG1 (+), S100 (+), SOX9 (+). H3F3B K36M mutation (>90%).
Osteoid Osteoma vs. Osteoblastoma 10–30y; OO: Cortex of long bones (<2 cm, nocturnal pain relieved by NSAIDs). OB: Posterior spine (>2 cm) Interlacing trabeculae of woven bone rimmed by a single layer of plump benign osteoblasts in a richly vascular loose stroma (no cartilage, no atypical mitoses) FOS & FOSB nuclear IHC (+), SATB2 (+). Driven by FOS (85%) or FOSB (15%) rearrangements (negative in osteosarcoma!).
Enchondroma vs. Chondrosarcoma (Gr 1–3) Enchondroma: Hands/feet or central bone (<5 cm). Chondrosarcoma: Pelvis, femur, ribs (>50y, endosteal scalloping) Enchondroma: Hyaline cartilage lobules encased by lamellar bone shells. Chondrosarcoma: Permeative growth trapping pre-existing host lamellar bone on all sides & Haversian invasion S100 (+), SOX9 (+), IDH1 R132 or IDH2 mutation (50–70% of central/periosteal; ABSENT in peripheral osteochondroma-derived!).
Parosteal vs. Conventional Osteosarcoma Conventional: 10–25y, metaphysis, sunburst/Codman triangle. Parosteal: 20–40y, posterior distal femur ('stuck-on' surface mass) Conventional: Pleomorphic malignant cells producing lacy osteoid. Parosteal (Low-grade): Parallel streamers of bone trabeculae in bland fibroblastic stroma (mimics fibrous dysplasia!) SATB2 (+). Parosteal OS & Low-Grade Central OS: MDM2 and CDK4 amplification (12q13–15) in >85% (distinguishes from Fibrous Dysplasia!).
Fibrous Dysplasia vs. Ossifying Fibroma vs. COD • FD: Poorly defined 'ground-glass' blending into bone.
• OF: Mandible; well-circumscribed corticated lesion (shells out cleanly).
• COD: Tooth apex.		 • Fibrous Dysplasia: Curvilinear ('Chinese character') woven bone WITHOUT osteoblastic rimming.
• Ossifying Fibroma: Bone trabeculae & cementum-like spherules WITH prominent osteoblastic rimming.		 • Fibrous Dysplasia: Activating missense mutation in GNAS (R201H/C) (absent in OF and COD!).
1. SOE Data-Gathering:
Request patient age/physis status, plain radiograph/MRI compartment (epiphyseal vs. metaphyseal), serum Calcium/Phosphate/PTH (to rule out Brown tumor of hyperparathyroidism), and prior denosumab therapy.
2. SOE Reasoning (IHC/Mol):
Order H3.3 G34W (positive in >90% GCTB) and H3.3 K36M (Chondroblastoma), plus USP6 FISH for primary ABC or MDM2 for low-grade osteosarcoma.
3. SOE Decision-Making:
Recognize that neoadjuvant denosumab depletes osteoclast giant cells and induces woven bone deposition mimicking osteosarcoma—confirming H3.3 G34W prevents catastrophic overtreatment.
4. SOE Professional & QC:
Decalcify bone biopsy fragments in EDTA rather than strong acid to preserve H3.3 G34W/K36M antigenicity and DNA/RNA integrity, and correlate at the orthopedic oncology multidisciplinary board.
