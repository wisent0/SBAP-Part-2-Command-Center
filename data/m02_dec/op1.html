One-Pager 1: Adipocytic, Fibroblastic/Myofibroblastic & Myxoid Soft Tissue Tumors (WHO 5th Ed.)

Surgical Path (50%) + IHC/Molecular (10%) + Day 2 SOE

1. Etiology, Molecular Pathogenesis & Epidemiology: Soft tissue sarcomas account for <1% of adult malignancies. WHO 5th Edition classifies them by recurrent driver alterations: (a) 12q13–15 amplification (MDM2 and CDK4) in Atypical Lipomatous Tumor (ALT) / Well-Differentiated Liposarcoma (WDLPS) and Dedifferentiated Liposarcoma (DDLPS); (b) 13q14 deletion (RB1 loss) in the benign 13q family (Spindle Cell/Pleomorphic Lipoma, Myofibroblastoma); (c) FUS::DDIT3 t(12;16) [95%] in Myxoid Liposarcoma (deep thigh of young adults; metastasizes to serosal surfaces, fat pads, and spine); (d) USP6 rearrangements (17p13) in self-limiting lesions (Nodular Fasciitis, Myositis Ossificans, Aneurysmal Bone Cyst); (e) CTNNB1 mutations in Desmoid Fibromatosis; and (f) NAB2::STAT6 fusion in Solitary Fibrous Tumor (SFT).
